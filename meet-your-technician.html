HOW IT ALL STARTED

HEY, I'M REMY.

The carpet cleaner, business owner, husband, and dad behind the name.

Before Clean & Green, I worked as a technician for another great local carpet cleaning company. I really enjoyed the work—and especially the people I got to meet along the way.

Then I spent time serving at a refugee center in Romania. When I came home, I was ready for a fresh start, so I returned to what I knew and loved: cleaning carpets. That's how Clean & Green began.

Since then, life has grown in the best ways. I got married, we welcomed our first son, and we're getting ready for another little one. The business has grown too, adding more ways to care for our neighbors' homes.

More than three years in, what I love most hasn't changed: getting to know the people who invite us into their homes.

Thanks for being part of our story. — Remy
