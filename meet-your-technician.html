A NOTE FROM THE OWNER

Hey, I'm Remy!

Thanks for choosing Clean & Green. I wanted to say hello before we meet.

I got my start working as a technician for another great local carpet cleaning company. After spending time doing missions work at a refugee center in Romania, I came home ready for a fresh start.

So I went back to what I knew and loved: cleaning carpets.

A lot has changed since then! I got married, we welcomed our first little boy, and now we've got another on the way. Clean & Green has grown beyond carpet cleaning, too.

Over three years in, my favorite part is still the people I get to work with.

Thanks again for trusting me with your home. I look forward to meeting you!

— Remy
